# Frequently asked questions

## What is Gym Master?

It is a platform designed to connect management, operations, and the member experience within one system.

## Which profiles is it designed for?

The public experience is organized around Administrator, Team / Commercial, and Member profiles.

## Does it manage members and membership fees?

Yes. The public product scope includes members, membership fees, and payments within a connected operation.

## Does it include sales and stock?

Yes. Sales and stock are part of the publicly communicated operational and commercial workflow.

## Does it include access or attendance?

Yes. Gym Master includes identification, check-in, access validation, and attendance as part of the operational experience.

## Does it include workout routines and progress?

Yes. The member experience connects routines, sessions, records, and progress.

## Does it include diets and medical records?

Yes. Both are part of the public member follow-up capabilities.

## Does it provide reports or connected information views?

Yes. Gym Master connects operational and business information to provide a more integrated view.

## Does it use artificial intelligence to predict results?

The available public information does not confirm automatic prediction of results. Signals shown in the promotional experience are conceptual and illustrative.

## How much does it cost?

The public corpus does not define pricing. For specific commercial information, request a demo or use the available commercial contact channel.

## Can I request a demo?

Yes. A public demo-request flow is available in Spanish and English.
