# Team and commercial operations

Gym Master connects everyday team actions to reduce friction between operational steps.

A typical flow can include:

1. member arrival;
2. identification or check-in;
3. access validation;
4. payment or membership fee;
5. sale;
6. stock update;
7. operation record.

## Operational continuity

The idea is for each action to carry context into the next.

For example, a payment, sale, or stock movement is not presented as an isolated event, but as part of one operational thread.

## Goal

The goal is to reduce unnecessary friction and manual work so the team can focus on resolving the member interaction.

## Promotional data

Times, statuses, names, values, and sequences shown in the promotional experience are illustrative.

They do not represent real production data.
