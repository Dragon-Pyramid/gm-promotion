# Member

Gym Master is designed to maintain continuity in the member experience from entry through training and follow-up.

## Entry

The arrival experience can connect:

- identification;
- member status;
- access validation;
- gym entry.

The goal is for the system to handle this context without turning arrival into unnecessary paperwork.

## Training and progress

Gym Master connects:

- workout routine;
- session execution;
- record;
- progress.

This allows today's training to become part of a visible progress history for the member.

## Continuity

The relationship can continue after the session through:

- follow-up;
- messages;
- reminders;
- context for the next visit.

## Limits of this description

Names, times, plans, percentages, exercises, and statuses shown in the promotional experience are illustrative.

They do not represent real member data and do not imply guaranteed physical results.
