# Member follow-up and relationship

Gym Master is designed to preserve context from one visit to the next.

After training, continuity can be supported through:

- follow-up;
- messages;
- notifications;
- reminders;
- next visit;
- context from the previous workout.

## Goal

Communication is not presented as additional noise.

The purpose is to help the gym support the member using context from what already happened and what comes next.

## Scope

The promotional experience shows illustrative examples of messages, reminders, and statuses.

It does not promise full automation and does not imply that every communication is generated autonomously.
