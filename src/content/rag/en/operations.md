# Daily operations

Gym Master is designed to maintain continuity from member arrival through the final record of each operational action.

A workflow can connect:

- identification;
- check-in;
- membership status;
- payment;
- sale;
- stock movement;
- activity record.

## Cash desk, sales, and stock

Commercial operations can connect sales and stock movements within the same operational context.

The goal is to reduce duplicated work and prevent each step from becoming an isolated process.

## Purchasing, services, and internal operations

The platform also covers areas related to purchasing and replenishment, suppliers, services, promotions, expenses, and equipment.

These capabilities form part of a broader connected gym operation.
