# Administrator

Gym Master gives administrators an integrated view of what is happening across the gym.

The goal is to prevent members, collections, attendance, activity, and alerts from being scattered across separate tools or records.

## Operational view

Administrators can work with connected information related to:

- active members;
- collections;
- attendance;
- alerts;
- recent activity.

The value is not only in seeing data, but in understanding it within one operational context.

## Business view

Gym Master also connects information from:

- membership fees;
- sales;
- services;
- expenses.

This helps turn daily activity into a more integrated view of the business.

## Limits of this description

Metrics, percentages, values, and visual examples shown in the promotional experience are illustrative.

They do not represent real customer data and do not guarantee specific business results.
