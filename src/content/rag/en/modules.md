# Functional areas

Gym Master organizes different parts of the gym inside one connected experience.

Publicly communicated capabilities include:

- members;
- membership fees and payments;
- attendance and access;
- sales;
- stock;
- purchasing and replenishment;
- services and promotions;
- expenses;
- suppliers;
- equipment and maintenance;
- trainers and schedules;
- activities;
- workout routines;
- diets;
- physical progress;
- medical records;
- notifications and messages;
- reports and connected information views.

The goal is not to treat these areas as independent systems, but to preserve context between them.

## Profiles

Capabilities are experienced differently by each profile:

- Administrator: control and integrated business visibility.
- Team / Commercial: everyday operations.
- Member: access, training, and follow-up.

The presence of a functional area does not mean every profile has the same permissions or interface.
