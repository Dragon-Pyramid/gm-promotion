# Training and progress

Gym Master connects the member's workout routine with execution, records, and progress.

The member experience can relate:

- workout routine;
- exercises;
- current session;
- training loads or records;
- session history;
- physical progress.

## Continuity

The goal is for each session not to start from zero.

Today's training remains connected to previous work and to the next objective.

## Diets and medical record

The member experience also includes diets and a medical record as part of the broader follow-up context.

## Illustrative data

Percentages, loads, weeks, exercises, and visual examples used in the promotional experience are illustrative.

They do not represent real results and do not guarantee specific physical changes.
