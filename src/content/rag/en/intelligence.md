# Connected information view

Gym Master relates information from different areas to provide a clearer view of the gym.

Sources that can share context include:

- attendance;
- payments;
- sales;
- training;
- member relationship.

## Signals

The promotional experience uses concepts such as:

- rhythm;
- change;
- attention.

These signals represent a way to organize and read connected information.

They must not be interpreted as automatic predictions, autonomous decisions, or guaranteed analytics.

## Goal

The value lies in reducing fragmentation and helping interpret what is happening through shared context across different areas.
