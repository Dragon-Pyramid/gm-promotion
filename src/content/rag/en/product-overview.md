# Gym Master

Gym Master is a platform designed to connect gym management, daily operations, and the member experience within one system.

Its purpose is to reduce fragmented information across members, payments, attendance, sales, stock, training, and follow-up.

Instead of treating each process as an isolated task, Gym Master is designed to preserve context from one action to the next.

## What it connects

Gym Master brings together areas such as:

- members;
- membership fees and payments;
- attendance and access;
- sales;
- stock;
- services;
- expenses;
- training;
- member progress;
- follow-up and communication.

## Who it is for

The public Gym Master experience is organized around three profiles:

- Administrator: needs a clear view of operations and the business.
- Team / Commercial: needs continuity across everyday operational tasks.
- Member: needs a simple experience from entry to training and follow-up.

## Core idea

Gym Master is not presented as a collection of disconnected modules.

The goal is for the different parts of the gym to share context so management, operations, and the member experience work together.
